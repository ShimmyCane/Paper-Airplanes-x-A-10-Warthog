Missions Mode: what to expect

Ten missions in order. Each one has a goal, a start, and a set of fighters and guns. The first flight is a calm practice run, and the later ones are harder.

A mission, step by step

1

Read the card

The mission name and a short brief show at the start. The top bar shows how many targets are left.

2

Find the targets

Arrows on the radar point to targets off the screen. Fly the arrow, then find the target.

3

Survive the waves

Fighters come in from ahead in waves. A warning tells you when a missile is locked on you.

4

Finish and restock

Reach the goal to earn 500 points. Ammo, missiles, chaff and hull are restored, and the next mission starts.

What fights back

Enemy fighters
They fire guns and homing missiles. A warning tag shows the missile's distance. A hard turn or chaff can break the lock.
Your allies
They fly around their own patch and do not shoot. Hitting one costs points, and lost allies are replaced.
Guns on the ground
Tanks shoot with machine guns, and flak bursts up at you. SAM sites fire missiles. Only missions with guns have them, which is every mission from Coast Watch on, except Intercept and Heading Home.
Ships and route bases
Ships fire cannon, and defended bases with SAM and flak come up along the route. Both only appear in missions with guns.

Your tools

Throttle
The lever, left of the buttons, or R and F. It sets your top speed. At the default 60% you level off near 125 knots, and at full power about 150.
Flight helper
On by default. It keeps the nose short of a stall and adds power when you are slow. Turn it off in settings for real flying.
Guns and missiles
Hold the nose on a target until it locks. Missiles home on a locked target.
Chaff and reload
Chaff breaks a missile's lock for a few seconds. The ⚙️ reload restocks you, and can be used once every 45 seconds.

Difficulty and settings

Felix (easy)

The plane never goes down. Supplies are unlimited. Fighter waves are halved again.

Normal

Enough hits and the plane goes down. The mission restarts with supplies refilled.

Enemy fighters per wave

The setting scales each mission's waves. The default is 1 (half). Set 0 for no fighters.

After mission 10

The list starts again. Each time round, every mission asks for one more target, and the waves get one fighter bigger. The missions stay in the same order, so Range Day comes back as a little harder version of itself.

Numbers come from the game's mission table in version 0.22. Escort, carrier launches, team battles and bomber intercepts are planned for later.
